### 4.1 Act 00 — the opening you approved

Full-bleed hero, `WhatsApp Image…11.54.24` cropped to 16:9, pillarbox removed.
`filter: blur(18px)` + `scale(1.08)` behind a wax seal. On open: blur resolves to
0 over 1.2s, seal cracks, her name rises. Text column occupies the **left 0–55%**
— her face sits at ~64% in the cropped frame, so the type never crosses it.

### 4.2 Act 03 — "Both Sides", rebuilt

**v1 was wrong.** I designed an abstract draggable split-screen divider. Having
now seen `#7`–`#10` and `#2`, that metaphor is unnecessary — you already have
real frames of the two of you together, and a real frame is always stronger than
a constructed one.

**v2 concept: "Two Rooms."**

A pinned, full-height act. Two columns:

- **Left — him.** His side of the video call, taken *from the screenshots*: a
  cropped, enlarged inset lifted out of `#7`/`#8`/`#9`/`#10`. His room. The
  window behind him. Underneath, his voice in first person.
- **Right — her.** Her side, full-frame, from the same screenshots.

**The interaction:** as she scrolls, the two images slide toward each other and
**lock into a single frame** — the two insets from a video call finally meeting.
Scrolling back pulls them apart again. Scrubbed to scroll position, so she
controls the pace by how fast she moves.

**Why this beats v1:**
- It uses real material, not an invented UI metaphor.
- It *is* the long-distance story — two rooms, one call.
- The "lock together" moment is the emotional peak of the act, and it produces
  the screenshot she'll share.
- The two best frames (`#9` + `#10`) are from the same room and the same moment
  — **you are both laughing in `#9`**. That is the frame the act builds to.

Keyboard and touch accessible. Reduced-motion gets a static side-by-side.

### 4.3 Act 07 — the keepsake

Canvas-rendered card: her name, the date, one line from the letter, a thin brass
rule, on paper texture. Offered as a download. This is what she keeps, sets as a
lock screen, and posts. It is also the highest-value organic-sharing mechanic in
the design.

## 8d. P3 COMPLETE — Act 02 (The Distance) and Act 03 (Both Sides)

### Act 02 — The Distance

Two live clocks and the honest part. The gap is **computed from the IANA zones at
runtime**, not hardcoded: `Asia/Kathmandu` is UTC+5:45 and `Asia/Bangkok` is
UTC+7, so it prints **1h 15m apart**. The normaliser folds a ±19h date-line
wrap, so it can never print "19h 15m". Ticks are aligned to the minute boundary
so the seconds do not visibly lag. A server-rendered `--:--` fallback means the
no-JS page shows a dash rather than a lie.

Copy note: "1h 15m **behind**" was the first wording and it was ambiguous about
*which* clock was behind, with the two clocks either side of it. Changed to
"apart", which is true either way.

### Act 03 — Both Sides

Built from the real video-call frames, not the abstract split v1 proposed.
`tools/p3-crops.mjs` measures the caller's tile off a coordinate grid
(`tools/build/inset-grid.png`) and crops it at native resolution:

| Crop | Source | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `his-laugh.webp` | the caller tile, lifted out of `call-3` | 167×305, 7 KB |
| `her-laugh.webp` | her, same frame, same call | 462×693, 13 KB |

**The tile is deliberately not enlarged.** A 167px-wide video-call inset blown
up to fill half a 1296px screen is a 4× upscale and looks like mush. Treating it
as a call tile is both the honest reading of what it is and the only way it stays
sharp. Below `60rem` it drops to 54% of the half-column, which on a 390px phone
is ~105px — still legibly a video tile.

**The scrub**, measured rather than assumed (values read after the 0.55s easing
settled, at five scroll positions):

| Progress | Gap between rooms | Tile scale | Seam text | Seam rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.00 | 205 px | 0.90 | 0.00 | 0 px |
| 0.25 | 80 px | 1.00 | 0.00 | 0 px |
| 0.50 | **0 px — met** | 1.06 | 0.00 | 0 px |
| 0.75 | 0 px | 1.06 | 0.39 | 32 px |
| 1.00 | 0 px | 1.06 | **1.00** | 54 px |

Pinning is CSS `position: sticky`, not GSAP `pin` — `pin` injects a spacer and
reflows the page; sticky does the same job with no layout shift and no hard
dependency on GSAP having loaded. `x` uses percentage strings so it re-resolves
correctly on resize for free. `document.fonts.ready` triggers
`ScrollTrigger.refresh()`, because late-arriving fonts change every act's
measured height and the scrub would otherwise end against the wrong layout.

### Bugs found and fixed in P3

1. **The seam caption could never appear.** `.bs-seam` had `opacity: 0` in CSS
   and GSAP only animated the *child* `.bs-seam__text`. The parent clamped the
   child to invisible regardless of the tween. Visibility moved to the child.
2. **Place labels were invisible.** Animated `from opacity: 0.35` — too dim to
   read. They are children of the sides and already travel with them, so the
   opacity tween was deleted outright rather than brightened.
3. **No-JS visitors scrolled through 2.4 screens of nothing.** The `340vh`
   runway existed to give the scrub somewhere to travel, but without JS there is
   no scrub. Scoped to `.js`; the act is now a normal full-screen section.
4. **Horizontal overflow on mobile** (411px of content in a 390px viewport).
   The `white-space: nowrap` gap label could not shrink, forcing the three-column
   clock grid past the edge. Fixed with `min-width: 0` on the grid children, a
   smaller `clamp()` floor, and a two-column stack below 30rem. Verified zero
   overflow and zero `scrollX` drift at five scrub positions.
5. **Two contrast failures, caught by Lighthouse dropping 100 → 96.**

## 8g. P6 + P7 COMPLETE — hardening and deploy readiness

### The one bug that would have shipped broken

**`og:image` was a relative URL.** Open Graph requires absolute URLs — WhatsApp,
Facebook and X fetch that string with a bare HTTP request and do **not** resolve
it against the page. A relative `./assets/img/og.jpg` therefore yields a link
preview with **no image at all**, which is the single most important thing on a
site whose entire purpose is being pasted into a chat. Now absolute, alongside
`og:url`, `twitter:image`, `og:site_name`, `og:locale`, `og:image:alt` and a
canonical link. If the deploy path changes, that block is the only thing to edit.

### Other P6 fixes

1. **Focus ring failed on the dark acts.** `.act--dark` was referenced in a CSS
   focus rule but never applied to any element, so Act 05 and Act 08 fell back to
   `--brass-deep` on `--ink` — **2.75:1, under the 3:1 an indicator needs**.
   `.act--dark` is now on `act-voice` and `act-close`; the ring resolves to
   `--brass-light` at **9.89:1**.
2. **404 on the Act 05 video poster.** The HTML pointed at
   `./assets/img/message-main.webp`; the file is in `./assets/video/`. The
   integrity checker had only ever scanned `src` and `href`, so it reported a
   clean page while the emotional peak of the site was showing a black box.
   Both fixed: the path, and the checker now reads `poster` and `data-src` too.
3. **Dead, already-stale second source of truth.** `config.js` exported an
   `ASSETS` block mirroring the hero `srcset`. Nothing imported it, and it had
   already drifted — three widths against the HTML's five. Deleted; the HTML is
   the single source of truth, which it has to be for the preload hint and the
   no-JS path.
4. **Orphaned focus on seal open.** Focus sat on the gate, which then became
   `display: none`, dropping a keyboard user to `<body>`. Focus is now handed to
   `#letter` on open, and "Read it again" replays properly.
5. **Toggle semantics.** The sound button changed its visible text *and* carried
   `aria-pressed`; pinning `aria-label="Sound"` over changing text then tripped
   axe's `label-content-name-mismatch`. Settled on the correct toggle pattern:
   visible label is always "Sound", state is `aria-pressed`, off-state is shown
   by the crossed speaker glyph. When autoplay is refused — every iOS browser —
   the control nudges twice instead of a modal appearing.
